Recovery Promise

ClearClean Recovery Promise
What happens if a must-do in your Quality Agreement isn't met. Applies to every visit booked with an agreement.

Covered

A must-do in your Quality Agreement that wasn't done
A must-do done clearly below what was agreed
Damage to belongings during the visit, handled by a support specialist

Not covered

Items marked “can't do” with a reason, or a change you approved. They move to your next visit.
Tasks listed under “Won't be done” in your Quality Agreement
Reports made more than 24 hours after the visit ends

How it works

1Report within 24 hours. Pick the agreement item and add a photo if you can.
2First reply within 2 hours (08:00–21:00).
3Review. Your photo is compared with the Tasker's after-photo and the agreement item, and she can add her side. Decision within 24 hours.
4Outcome. A re-clean of that item within 48 hours, or a partial refund. Track every step in the app.

Each case is checked against your Quality Agreement, so a re-clean or refund isn't automatic. Proposed policy for the pilot, to be confirmed against beHome's current service rules.
